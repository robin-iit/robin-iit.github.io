About

I am a mechanical engineering researcher working in computational fluid dynamics. My doctoral research investigated droplets in gaseous flows. I am interested in understanding interfacial dynamics and extending computational approaches to a wider range of gas–liquid flow problems.

Currently, I am working as a postdoctoral fellow at the Department of Applied Mechanics of the Indian Institute of Technology Madras.

Research interests

Droplet dynamics in gaseous flows

Deformation, breakup, and transport of droplets subjected to gaseous flows, including the effects of flow unsteadiness and fluid rheology.

Gas–liquid flows in wells and pipelines

Numerical investigation of gas–liquid flow structures and flow regimes, including slug flow in confined geometries relevant to petroleum applications.

Numerical methods for multiphase flows

Interface-capturing methods, volume-of-fluid formulations, and adaptive mesh refinement for computational multiphase-flow simulations.
